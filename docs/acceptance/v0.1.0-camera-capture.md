The hardware acceptance was confirmed by the project owner after compiling and
flashing the firmware from the accepted source state.

## Accepted Capabilities

- OV5640 initializes on the verified board wiring.
- The firmware serves a native 128x128 JPEG through `/capture`.
- The firmware serves an MJPEG preview through `/stream` on port 81.
- `/api/status` reports camera, stream, capture, heap, and PSRAM state.
- The local capture console pauses the preview before continuous still capture.
- Captured JPEGs are written locally with atomic file replacement.
- `metadata.csv` records each saved frame.
- Stopping capture restores the MJPEG preview.
